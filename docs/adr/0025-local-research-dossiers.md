# Full research dossiers stay local and are not committed

Full dossiers under `research/` stay in the local workspace and are ignored by Git. A dossier contains large compiler outputs and raw provider payloads (tens of megabytes per contract), and it can be regenerated from the target registry plus a pinned chain and block. Committing every dossier makes the repository impractically large and duplicates evidence that the committed fixture/golden files and reproducible commands already anchor. The repository therefore commits the target registry, design docs, ADRs, schemas, the skill, and fixture/golden evidence, while `research/` remains the local, inspectable record of a run. A fresh checkout has no dossiers and must rerun the CLI; sharing a dossier requires an explicit archive or a separate evidence store. Git LFS or an external evidence store can be reconsidered if sharing full dossiers becomes a real requirement.
